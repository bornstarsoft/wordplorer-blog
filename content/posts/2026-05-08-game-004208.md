The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, a captivating new puzzle game that challenges players to engage in lateral thinking and discover hidden connections. With its unique format of four rows of four tiles, players must identify three words that share a common link while spotting the imposter that doesnt belong. This innovative approach to puzzles not only entertains but also sharpens cognitive skills, making it a must-try for puzzle enthusiasts.

Each day presents a fresh challenge, with new puzzles released at midnight, encouraging players to return daily and compete with friends. The games clever design promotes critical thinking and creativity, as players must think outside the box to uncover the connections between seemingly unrelated words. As you dive into Relink, youll find that each puzzle is a delightful exercise for the mind, perfect for anyone looking to enhance their problem-solving abilities.
